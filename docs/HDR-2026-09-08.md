# HDR investigation — 2026-09-08

## Question

Determine whether HDR improves the LG UltraGear display path and whether it is the cause of the earlier pale image.

## Baseline

The final SDR profile is 2560×1440 at 239.970 Hz, scale 1.6, 10-bit `XRGB2101010`, VRR on, DPMS on and `cm=srgb`. The DP link is Good and the connector reports VRR capability. Hyprland's default color-management policy is active: `render:cm_enabled=true`, `render:cm_auto_hdr=1`, `render:cm_sdr_eotf=default`.

## Reversible experiment

The test kept resolution, refresh rate, scale, bit depth and VRR constant and changed only the monitor color-management preset.

| Preset | Hyprland state | DRM colorspace | HDR metadata | Link | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `srgb` | `colorManagementPreset=srgb` | Default | inactive SDR metadata | Good | Stable SDR desktop |
| `wide`/`auto` | `colorManagementPreset=wide` | BT2020_RGB | no PQ metadata | Good | Previously associated with pale SDR output |
| `hdr` | `colorManagementPreset=hdr` | BT2020_RGB | PQ (`eotf=2`) | Good | HDR path engaged; 603 cd/m² peak metadata |

During the forced HDR sample, Hyprland reported 239.970 Hz, scale 1.6, 10-bit and VRR on. DRM reported:

```text
Colorspace: BT2020_RGB
HDR EOTF: SMPTE 2084 / PQ
max_display_mastering_luminance: 603
max_cll: 603
max_fall: 276
max bpc: 10
link-status: Good
```

The test restored `cm=srgb` automatically. The final state was 240 Hz, scale 1.6, 10-bit, VRR on, DPMS on and no mirror.

## What HDR can improve

HDR content can use PQ transfer, BT2020 primaries, a wider gamut and higher highlight headroom. This panel path exposes about 603 cd/m² peak metadata, so HDR video and games with correct HDR signaling may show brighter highlights and more saturated colors while retaining 10-bit output.

## What HDR can worsen

For normal SDR applications, forcing `cm=hdr` makes the compositor tone-map SDR content into HDR. The result depends on the source and luminance mapping; the desktop can become dim, gray, oversaturated or washed out. HDR may also affect screenshots, screen sharing, color-managed applications and fullscreen transitions. Hyprland documents `cm=hdr` as experimental and recommends using auto-HDR for compatible fullscreen content. Recent Hyprland reports describe washed-out or clipped HDR output on other GPUs and panels, so those reports are a risk signal rather than proof of a failure on this AMD/LG path.

## Decision

Keep the persistent desktop rule at `cm = "srgb"`. Keep the default `render:cm_auto_hdr=1` policy so an HDR-aware fullscreen application can request HDR. Do not force `cm=hdr`, `supports_hdr` or manual luminance overrides globally until a real HDR video or game is tested and visually verified.

The pale image was isolated to the SDR `cm=auto`/`wide` path, not to 240 Hz. The current persistent profile is therefore 240 Hz + 10-bit + sRGB desktop + auto-HDR policy.
